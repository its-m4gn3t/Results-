MAD MORANS ESPORTS Team Rules and Regulations

1. Attendance and Punctuality

  • All team members must join practices, scrims, and meetings on time.
  • Notify the team leader or coach at least 24 hours in advance if unable to attend.
  • Habitual tardiness or unexcused absences may result in disciplinary action or benching.

2. Communication Protocol

  • Use the official team communication platform (voice chat, Discord, etc.) during sessions.
  • Maintain respectful and clear communication at all times.
  • Avoid unnecessary chatter; focus on concise callouts and strategic discussions.
  • Report technical issues promptly and switch channels if communication fails.

3. Practice Conduct and Focus

  • Stay fully engaged during practice and scrims. No multitasking or distractions (phones outside of gameplay, unrelated browsing).
  • Follow the planned training schedule and respect the coach's instructions.
  • Use constructive language; avoid blame, negativity, or toxic behavior.

4. Role Commitment

  • Stick to assigned roles during matches unless changed by team leadership.
  • Train to maintain versatility but respect role specialization.
  • Communicate openly if role changes or issues arise.

5. Accountability and Improvement

  • Review scrim/game footage regularly and participate actively in feedback sessions.
  • Set personal improvement goals aligned with team objectives.
  • Accept constructive criticism professionally and work on weaknesses proactively.

6. Equipment and Game Settings

  • Maintain personal gaming hardware and software for optimal performance.
  • Agree on standardized game settings for fairness during team play.
  • Report any hardware or connectivity issues affecting performance immediately.

7. Conflict Resolution

  • Address conflicts internally with maturity and respect.
  • Escalate unresolved issues to team leadership or coach calmly and privately.
  • Foster a positive and supportive team environment.

8. Professionalism and Representation

  • Represent the team positively in all public and online interactions.
  • Follow any sponsorship, social media, and branding guidelines provided.
  • Avoid any conduct that may harm the team's reputation.

9. Consequences

  • Warnings for minor breaches with possibility of temporary benching.
  • Repeated violations may lead to removal from the active roster.
  • Serious misconduct (cheating, harassment) results in immediate suspension or termination.
